I am a PhD student in Geophysics in the [Division of Geological and Planetary Sciences](https://www.gps.caltech.edu/) at Caltech, working with Paul Asimow and Mike Gurnis.

I use numerical models and Bayesian inversion to study the thermal and mechanical evolution of planetary interiors, from subduction zones on Earth to the deep mantle of Mars and the ice shell of Europa.

**Current research**

- **Mars:** Is there a molten layer at the base of the Martian mantle? I combine thermodynamic modeling with seismic observations from InSight to find out.
- **Subduction zones:** What controls the strength of subducting plates? I use adjoint-based Bayesian inversion of geodynamic models to infer subduction-zone rheology from surface observations.

See [Research](/research/) for details.
